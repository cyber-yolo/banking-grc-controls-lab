# RA-001 Log retention of 90 days

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Control | CTL-010 |
| Requirements affected | REQ-CH-11 (Swiss DPO logging, retention believed to be one year, TO VERIFY), REQ-AIA-05 (AI Act deployer logs, at least six months, TO VERIFY), REQ-RMF-03 |
| Gap | Workspace retention is 90 days |
| Reason | Retention above 90 days is billed; the lab runs on free offers only and lasts about 30 days, so no log can reach 90 days anyway |
| Compensating measures | Evidence exported and hashed before teardown (docs/08-evidence-handling.md) |
| What a bank would do | Interactive retention per its logging standard, plus archive or export to low-cost storage for the legal period |
| Decision by | Lab maintainer (acting as risk owner) |
| Date | YYYY-MM-DD |
| Expires | End of the lab campaign |
